## 1. Procurement Services

**Tagline:** Global sourcing, delivered locally.

CABTECNI is an Angolan worldwide sourcing partner built around a single objective: getting you the right equipment and materials, at the right price, on time. We manage the full procurement cycle — sourcing, ordering, expediting, monitoring, and delivery — so your operations never stall waiting on parts.

Backed by our South African headquarters, NAS Global (Pty) Ltd, we combine international supplier access with on-the-ground knowledge of the Angolan market, customs, and logistics.

**What we deliver:**
- Full-cycle procurement: sourcing, RFQs, purchase order management, and expediting
- Access to a vetted global network of OEMs and approved suppliers
- Cost-effective sourcing without compromising quality or lead time
- Order tracking and status reporting from placement to delivery
- Import, customs clearance, and documentation handled end to end

## 2. Logistics

**Tagline:** From origin to site, safely and on schedule.

We move your goods from anywhere in the world to your operation in Angola — and to the most remote project sites within it. Our logistics team coordinates freight, warehousing, customs, and last-mile delivery as a single, accountable process, so goods arrive complete, on time, and in the condition they left.

**What we deliver:**
- International and domestic freight forwarding (air, sea, and road)
- Customs clearance and import/export documentation
- Warehousing, consolidation, and inventory handling
- Project and heavy-cargo logistics to remote and industrial sites
- Real-time shipment tracking and proactive delivery updates

## 3. Bolt Torquing & Tensioning

**Tagline:** Precision bolting for critical joints.

Leaking flanges and failed joints cost time, money, and safety. Our certified technicians deliver controlled hydraulic and pneumatic bolt torquing and tensioning to exact specifications, ensuring the integrity of pressure-critical connections across pipelines, wellheads, valves, and structural assemblies.

**What we deliver:**
- Hydraulic torquing and hydraulic tensioning services
- Controlled, calibrated tool application to engineered specifications
- Flange management and joint-integrity assurance
- On-site service for shutdowns, turnarounds, and new construction
- Full documentation and torque reporting for compliance

## 6. Electric Motors — Maintenance & Rewinding

**Tagline:** Get your motors back in service, built to last.

Downtime on a critical motor is downtime on your whole operation. Our workshop services diagnose, repair, rewind, and recondition electric motors of all sizes, restoring performance and extending service life — with testing to prove it before the motor returns to duty.

**What we deliver:**
- Diagnostics, fault-finding, and condition assessment
- Rewinding of AC and DC motors across a range of sizes
- Bearing replacement, reconditioning, and mechanical repairs
- Load and insulation testing before handover
- Preventive maintenance programmes to reduce unplanned failures

## 8. Piping Manufacturing Services

**Tagline:** Fabricated to spec, ready to install.

From spool fabrication to full piping assemblies, our workshop manufactures piping to your drawings and industry codes. We deliver accurate, quality-controlled fabrication that arrives ready to install — reducing on-site work, rework, and schedule risk.
